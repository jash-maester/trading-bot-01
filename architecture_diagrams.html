trading-bot-01

A daily, long-only paper-trading system for NSE stocks: what feeds it, how it decides, which models and RL agents were tried, and where it runs. Paper only; no real orders are ever placed.

01 · System

How a day's data becomes a portfolio

Nightly NSE files feed a frozen neural signal and a rule-based allocator. The same targets drive the live ₹1L book at 09:16 and an end-of-day replay against random books.

Signal model

  • TCN encoder over 60 days × 15 features for each of 504 stocks
  • Outputs predicted 5-day and 20-day returns per stock
  • Frozen: last trained on data to Jan 2022; refit only on 1 April
  • Ranks stocks well overall, but its top 30 do no better than random picks

Allocator and risk

  • Top 30 by predicted 20-day return, weighted by 1/volatility
  • Caps: 10% per stock, 25% per sector; 1% no-trade band
  • 30% turnover per rebalance (lifted for the first funding)
  • Volatility stop: exit below entry by 1×vol×√20, 5–30%

Two ways it is run

  • Live book: ₹1L, filled at the 09:16 Kite quote, marked every 15 min
  • Experiment (P2): same rules replayed on NSE closes vs 20 random books
  • Adaptive shadow (P3): refit + rebalance twice a month, vs its own randoms
  • No real orders anywhere; Kite is read-only

02 · Decision

What happens at 09:16

One run per weekday, idempotent: retries at 09:20 and 09:30 change nothing once a day's trade is written.

03 · Schedule

One trading day, IST

Spacing is to scale. The 21:25 refit only fires on the evening before a 1st or 16th rebalance.

04 · Models

Every model and RL agent, and its status

Reinforcement learning was built twice and neither version earned its place. The supervised signal is what trades today.

End-to-end PPO (M5–M7)

  • Actor-critic reads the whole market tensor and outputs weights
  • Encoders: MLP, GNN; FiLM regime conditioning; auxiliary return head
  • Validation Sharpe was anti-correlated with test (−0.86)
  • Its M5 gate never passed, so nothing built on it is used

Supervised signal (R4)

  • TCN trained walk-forward, 13 windows, 2005–2024
  • Rank IC gate passed: 20d +0.028, t 4.19
  • But top-30 ranks 12th of 21 against random books
  • Information sits in the bottom tail a long-only book can't use

PPO over the allocator (R6)

  • Steps once a month; observes 42 allocator-state numbers
  • Acts on 17 dims (K, budget, cash floor, 14 sector tilts)
  • +1.5 CAGR points in sample, −2 to −3 points out of sample
  • Diagnosis: 17 parameters vs ~4 independent 2-year periods

05 · Deployment

Where it runs

One Mac mini, one Docker VM. Postgres also runs but nothing in the daily loop writes to it, so it is left off.
